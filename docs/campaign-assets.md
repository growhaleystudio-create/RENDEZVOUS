# Landing page photography and prototype portraits

The landing page uses editorial stock photographs by Unsplash and Pexels contributors plus six generated portraits for the barber prototype. The stock images are not photographs of Rendezvous employees, customers, or locations. Generated portraits are synthetic demo imagery and do not depict or represent verified Rendezvous staff. Barber names, branches, and booking information remain fictional demo content. The generated six-person team photograph in the booking section is also synthetic.
